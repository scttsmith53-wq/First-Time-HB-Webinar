What You’ll Learn

Five things worth knowing before you buy.

This is practical, plain-English education designed to help you understand the process before the pressure of a live transaction begins.

1. What cash you may actually need

Down payment, closing costs, assistance options, and why the number is often different than buyers expect.

2. Build the right team early

Know who does what, who should be answering which questions, and when to bring people into the process.

3. What may be negotiable

Seller credits, rate buydowns, contract terms, and where flexibility can sometimes create a better outcome.

4. Where buyer leverage lives

Why the home getting the most attention is not always the best opportunity for you.

5. How inspections protect you

Use information to make better decisions before you fully commit.
